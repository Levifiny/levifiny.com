Who We Are

Levifiny Inc. is a software start-up. We design and build apps for all kinds of needs, each one focused on doing a single job well.

Our first app, Batchbell, is a multi-timer for cooks and bakers and is heading to the App Store. A personal finance tracker, where Levifiny began, is in development.

The Name

Levifiny, said lev-ih-FIN-ee, joins three ideas that guide everything we make.

  • Levi · LevitateTo rise above what weighs you down.
  • Fin · FinanceWhere we began: making money matters lighter.
  • Finy · FinishThe moment a job is done, and worth celebrating.

“Everyone deserves their own sunrise.” Our colours come from a sunrise, and so does our hope for every app we make.

How We Build

Calm by design

Clear screens and friendly words. Our apps help you stay on top of things without adding noise or alarm.

Private by default

We collect only what an app truly needs, and nothing at all when we can. Batchbell has no account and no server.

Made to finish

Tools that help you get the job done, and mark the moment when you do.
