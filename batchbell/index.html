Batchbell

Timers for everything in the oven at once.

Batchbell is a multi-timer for cooks and bakers running several bakes at once. Save each bake once, start it with a tap or its number, and every tray rings on its own.

Get support Privacy policy

How It Helps

  • Run as many timers as you have trays, each with its own sound, colour and picture.
  • Split a bake into stages, with a reminder between them such as “rotate the tray”.
  • Give a batch + min when it needs longer, and it stays one bake in History.
  • Start a timer by voice when your hands are full: say “scones”, or “new timer, pizza, 12 minutes”.
  • See every bake by day, and save any week as a PDF report.
  • Share your timers with a coworker through a code or a link, with no server in between.

Private by Design

No account, no server, no ads and no tracking. Batchbell collects no data: your timers, history and settings stay on your iPhone, and nothing leaves it unless you share a report or a timer link yourself.

Support

Answers to common questions. In the app, the same answers are in Settings, under FAQ and Support.

Why didn’t my timer ring?

With the phone locked or in another app, Batchbell rings through a notification. Notifications must be allowed, Silent mode off, the ringer volume up, and no Focus on. Use Test an alarm in Settings to check.

Does it ring if I close the app?

Yes. Each timer hands its alarms to the phone when it starts, so they ring even if Batchbell is closed or swiped away.

Does each timer ring with its own sound when the phone is locked?

Yes. Each timer rings with the sound you chose for it, in the app and on the Lock Screen. If you hear nothing, turn off Silent mode and turn up Ringtone and Alerts in the phone’s Settings, Sounds & Haptics.

What does + min do?

When a bake needs longer, tap + min on its card and pick +1, +2, +5 or your own number. The extra goes on the same batch, so History shows one bake.

How do I start a timer by voice?

On Now, tap the microphone and say the timer’s name or number, such as “scones” or “number one”. Batchbell asks “Start Scone, 18 minutes?”: say yes to start it now or no to stop it, and if you say nothing it starts by itself after 5 seconds. When several timers match, it reads them out: say the one you mean, or tap it.

Can I add a timer by voice?

Yes, for when there is no time to type. On Now, tap the microphone and say “new timer”, its name and how long, such as “new timer, pizza, 12 minutes”. Batchbell asks for anything missing, then starts it after 5 seconds unless you say no. Change the rest later in My Timers.

How do I share my timers with a coworker?

On My Timers, tap Share, choose the timers, then show the code for them to scan with the iPhone Camera (or Scan on My Timers), or tap Send link to send it by message. They see the timers first and add the ones they want. Nothing goes through a server: the timers are inside the code.

How long is History kept?

This week and the 4 full weeks before it. Older days are deleted when the app opens. Save a week’s PDF from Report to keep it.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. There is no account and no server. Timers, history and settings stay on your phone, and nothing leaves it unless you share a report or a timer link, or email us yourself.

Can I use Batchbell in French?

Yes. Choose Français under Language in Settings.
