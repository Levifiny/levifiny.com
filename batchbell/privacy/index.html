Batchbell Privacy Policy

For Batchbell for iPhone, made by Levifiny Inc. Last updated: November 21, 2026.

In short

Batchbell collects no data. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you make in Batchbell stays on your iPhone, and nothing leaves it unless you choose to share it.

What Stays on Your iPhone

Your saved timers, running timers, history and its notes, stage templates and settings are kept in a database inside the app on your iPhone. Levifiny cannot see them, and they are never sent to a server of ours, because Batchbell has none.

History is kept for the current week and the four full weeks before it; older days are deleted automatically when the app opens. You can clear history at any time in Settings, and deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Like any app’s data, it may be included in your iPhone’s own backups to iCloud or a computer. Those backups are kept by you and Apple, not by us.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

Batchbell uses the microphone and speech recognition only for voice start. When you tap the microphone on Now, it listens once for a timer’s name or number, or a new timer, and stops after a few seconds. It never listens in the background.

It uses Apple’s speech recognition. Where your iPhone supports it, recognition happens on the iPhone itself. Where it cannot, Apple’s speech service turns the audio into text, under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Batchbell does not record, save or send any audio. It uses the words only to find or add a timer, then lets them go. Its spoken answers, such as “Scone started”, come from the iPhone’s built-in voice.

Batchbell asks for these permissions only the first time you use voice start. You can turn them off at any time in the iPhone’s Settings; everything else keeps working.

Camera

Batchbell uses the camera only when you choose Scan on My Timers, to read a code of timers someone shared with you. It takes no photos, saves nothing and sends nothing. You can paste a link instead, and turn camera access off in the iPhone’s Settings.

Alarms and the Lock Screen

Alarms are local notifications that Batchbell schedules on your iPhone when a timer starts; no push server is involved. Running timers can also show on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island as a Live Activity, which is drawn on the phone from the app’s own data.

What You Choose to Share

  • Report PDFs. When you tap Save PDF on Report, the iPhone’s share sheet opens and you choose where the PDF goes, such as Files, Mail or Messages. It holds that week’s history and notes.
  • Timer links and codes. When you share timers, their details (names, numbers, times, stages, notes, pictures, sounds, colours and oven labels) are packed inside the link or QR code itself. They go only to the people you send or show them to.
  • Emails to us. If you write to us, we receive what you send. The Email us button in Batchbell adds the app version, build, iOS version and iPhone model so we can help; you can delete those lines before sending. We use your message only to answer you and do not keep it longer than needed. Our email is handled by Gmail (Google).

No Third Parties

Batchbell contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code. It does not use the advertising identifier, and it does not sell or share data with anyone. The only outside services involved are Apple’s own: the App Store, and speech recognition as described above.

Children

Batchbell is made for a general audience. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your Choices

Because Batchbell sends us nothing, we hold no data about you to look up, correct or delete. Your data is in your hands: edit or delete timers and history in the app, change permissions in the iPhone’s Settings, or delete the app to remove everything. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete your messages.

Changes to This Policy

If the way Batchbell handles data ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version of the app reaches the App Store.
