Batchbell

Coming soon to the App Store

iPhone · English and French · First Levifiny app

Batchbell is a multi-timer for cooks and bakers running several bakes at once. Save each bake once, start it with a tap, its number or your voice, and every tray rings on its own, even with the phone locked.

Timers can have stages such as “bake 7 minutes, rotate the tray, bake 7 more”, a batch that needs longer gets extra minutes and stays one bake, and History keeps a record of every batch by day, with a PDF report for any week.

Where Levifiny began

Our finance app grew out of a real problem: rent, bills, subscriptions and a student loan spread across notes, calendars and reminders, with no single view of what is due or how much is left.

It brings bills, subscriptions and loans into one calm place. It shows what is due and how each one gets paid, tracks every debt toward zero as freedom gained rather than balance remaining, and celebrates the “Finy” moment when a debt is paid off.
